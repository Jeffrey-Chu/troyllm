who we are

Service, but make it fun.

Key Club is the oldest and largest service program for high school students, sponsored by Kiwanis. At Troy, that means making care packages for seniors, cards with Joy4Juvies, and going to big events like Fall Rally South at Six Flags Magic Mountain, all in matching purple shirts.

FAQ

Who can join?

Any Troy High student. Join the Google Classroom and come to a lunch meeting in Room 517.

Are there dues?

Yes, yearly club dues. The payment form is in the Google Classroom and on our Linktree, and deadlines are announced on Instagram.

Do I need the club shirt?

The shirt is the attire for all Key Club events and is required for Fall Rally South. This year's shirt was $16. Watch Instagram for order windows and pickup times.

How do I earn service hours?

Come to service meetings and sign up for events in the Classroom. For example, at the senior care package meeting, every two bags you made counted as one service hour.

What is Fall Rally South?

The biggest Key Club event of the year, held at Six Flags Magic Mountain. Registration and payment happen through the Classroom and Linktree.

What's D30N?

Division 30 North, the group of nearby Key Clubs we belong to. Our mascot is the rhino.

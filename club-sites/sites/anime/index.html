About us

A safe haven for people who love anime.

The Anime Club encourages cultural expansion for our members and gives them a place to talk about shared interests with other club members.

It's a place to express your love for your favorite Japanese shows without worrying about being judged for having a niche interest.

Room 910
What we're about
01

Cultural expansion

Discover new shows, genres and pieces of Japanese culture you wouldn't have found on your own.

02

Shared interests

Talk theories, recs and favorite scenes with people who are just as into it as you are.

03

Zero judgment

Niche picks are welcome. Nobody here thinks your favorite is "too weird."

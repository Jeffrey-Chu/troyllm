Ritual & Care · 淨化保養

Keeping a stone clear

01 · 左進右出

Which wrist

Tradition says the left hand receives and the right hand releases. Wear wealth and love stones on the left; protective stones on the right.

02 · 月光淨化

Moon cleansing

Rest your stones on a windowsill under the full moon. Gentle and safe for every stone, including amethyst and rose tones that fade in sun.

03 · 音波淨化

Sound clearing

Ring a singing bowl or chime near the stone for a minute. Try the chime button on any product page.

04 · 保養

Daily care

Remove before showering, swimming or applying lotion. Store pieces apart so harder stones don't scratch softer ones.

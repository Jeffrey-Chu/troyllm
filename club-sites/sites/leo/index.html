About the club

Leo Clubs are the youth program of Lions Clubs International. At Troy, members earn volunteer hours at events, and attending meetings counts toward hours through meeting minutes.

Volunteering
Community events like Veterans Day, and floral decorating for the Lions International Rose Parade float over winter break.
Competitions
Leo contests like #HeartofaLeo, where we submitted a Project Kidney video.
Scholarships and grants
Announced at meetings and posted to Google Classroom.
CKD awareness
Outreach and videos about chronic kidney disease.

Recent events

Chronic kidney disease often goes unnoticed

CKD usually has no symptoms early on, so many people don't know they have it. About 1 in 10 people worldwide have it. Our outreach reel and the CKD × LEO promo video were made to get more people checked.

How to join

  1. Join the Google Classroom with code fwpiiobi. Announcements, sign-ups and requirements are posted there.
  2. Come to a meeting on the 1st or 3rd Friday at lunch. Check Instagram for the room.
  3. Sign up for events through the forms posted on Instagram and Classroom. The Troy LEO Calendar has the full schedule.

Common questions

Anything else? Message @troyhighleoclub.

Who can join?

Any Troy High student. Join the Classroom and come to a meeting.

Do meetings count as volunteer hours?

Yes. Attending earns meeting minutes that count as volunteer hours. Events count separately.

What is the Rose Parade float event?

Lions International enters a float in the Rose Parade. Leos help with floral decorating at Phoenix Decorating in Irwindale over winter break (Dec 26–31 last season). Requirements are on Google Classroom.

What is the IGEM interest form?

A form to gauge interest in IGEM. Fill out the IGEM Interest Form if you want to be involved.

    About the club.

    Troy Web Development started in 2023 as Web Development Education, where members taught each other how to build for the web. It still does that. Now most meetings go into real sites for real clubs.

    WHEN

    Every other Thursday

    Lunch, Room 411. Club news goes out on Google Classroom.

    WHAT

    Club websites

    Clubs ask us for a site. A member takes the lead, builds it with the club, and keeps it updated after launch.

    WHO

    Anyone at Troy

    No experience needed. Come to a meeting, pick a project, and learn by building it.

    YEARLY

    Tech Fair and Food Fest

    Picked for the Troy Tech Fair out of 125 clubs every year since 2023, and at Food Fest every year too.

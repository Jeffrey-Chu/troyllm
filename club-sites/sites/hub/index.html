About

About the club.

Troy Web Development started in 2023 as Web Development Education, a club where members taught each other how the web works. We still teach. Now most meetings go into building and updating real sites: for Troy clubs first, and for anyone else who asks.

When

Every other Thursday

Lunch in Room 411. Club news goes out on Google Classroom.

What

Real websites

Someone asks for a site, a member takes the lead, builds it with them, and keeps it updated after launch.

Who for

Not just Troy clubs

Nonprofits, student groups at other schools, a music collective, a small shop, even a biology game.

Who can join

Anyone at Troy

No experience needed. Come to a meeting, pick a project, and learn by building it.

How a site goes live

Every site we build lives in one GitHub repository. When a member pushes a change, it's checked and deployed to Firebase automatically, usually within a couple of minutes.

gitpushA member pushes a change
✓checkThe build and security rules are tested
fbdeployFirebase hosts the new version
●liveEveryone sees the update

Everyone came from somewhere.We write it down.

Roots & Roads is a student nonprofit at the Orange County School of the Arts. We interview immigrants in Orange County, film and write up their stories, and tutor students for free.

What we do

Interviews
We sit down with students and adults in our community and ask about their experience of immigration: what they left, how they got here, and what it's like being first-generation.
Free tutoring
Help with school and art, online or in person. Send us a message and we'll match you with a tutor.
Film and writing
Members film, edit and write each interview so it can be watched, read and kept. Anyone at OCSA can join.

Who we are

Roots & Roads shares the stories of immigrants in Orange County and supports students with resources, tutoring and a support network, so new immigrants feel at home wherever they are.

    What we believe

    Real and authentic

    We want to show what it's really like to be a first-generation immigrant, told by the people who lived it.

    The speaker leads

    The person telling the story decides what gets shared and how.

    Every story counts

    Students and adults, recent arrivals and long-time residents. Every journey is worth recording.

    Help should be free

    Our tutoring is free, online or in person, and matched to what you need.

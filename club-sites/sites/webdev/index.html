About the club.

What we are

Troy Web Development started in 2023 as Web Development Education, a club where members taught each other how the web works. We still teach. Now most meetings go into building and updating real sites: for Troy clubs first, and for anyone else who asks.

When

Every other Thursday

Lunch in Room 411. Club news goes out on Google Classroom.

What

Real websites

Someone asks for a site, a member takes the lead, builds it with them, and keeps it updated.

Who for

Not just Troy clubs

Nonprofits, student groups at other schools, a music collective, a small shop, a biology game.

Who can join

Anyone at Troy

No experience needed. Come to a meeting, pick a project, learn by building it.

Timeline

How we got here.

  1. 2023
    Web Development Education

    The club starts as a lecture club on HTML, CSS, JavaScript and how sites go live.

  2. 2023–25
    troywebdev.com and the club kiosk

    Our first site was a directory of Troy clubs used by more than 100 student organizations. Both it and the kiosk site are retired now.

  3. Every year
    Tech Fair and Food Fest

    Picked for the Troy Tech Fair out of 125 clubs every year since 2023, and at Food Fest every year too.

  4. 2026
    Thirteen sites live

    Troy clubs, a science nonprofit, an OCSA student group, a music collective, a crystal shop and a biology game. More in the works.

Process

How a site goes live.

Every site lives in one GitHub repository. When a member pushes a change, it's checked and deployed to Firebase automatically, usually within a couple of minutes.

01 · Pushgit pushA member pushes a change.
02 · Checkbuild ✓The build and security rules are tested.
03 · DeployfirebaseFirebase hosts the new version.
04 · Live200 OKEveryone sees the update.

Project Emotif · AI Life Guardian

Make brain states visible.

Project EMOTIF is a brain-computer interface research project. It turns EEG brainwave activity into real-time, AI-powered visual feedback, so changes in attention, arousal and relaxation reshape the world on screen. It doesn't try to read thoughts. The goal is to make brain states visible, interactive and trainable.

The headset

EMOTIF uses the EMOTIV Insight, a lightweight five-channel wireless EEG headset. It records from AF3, AF4, T7, T8 and Pz with semi-dry polymer sensors at 128 samples per second, plus a nine-axis motion sensor for head movement. Simple setup, Bluetooth and long battery life make it good for repeated testing.

What it could help with

  1. Reducing everyday stress and anxiety
  2. Better attention and concentration
  3. Stepping away from repetitive negative thoughts
  4. More confidence in situations that bring out shyness
  5. Calmer brain states before sleep

These are possible benefits; they still need to be tested in controlled research.

YAES

Young Aerospace Engineers Society

YAES brings together students who share a fascination with flight and space exploration. From SpaceX's reusable rockets and NASA's missions to China's space program and Blue Origin's spacecraft, advances in spaceflight are inspiring a new generation. Closer to home, engineers building flying cars and eVTOL aircraft are rethinking travel between and across cities. The Society is a place to explore the science behind these designs, share ideas, and imagine what you might build. YAES also hosts the annual National Engineering Bowl.

About & Join

A club for the scientifically obsessed.

The Science Club, "The Innovator Incubator," is a nonprofit for middle and high school students passionate about biology, chemistry, physics, astronomy, earth science, environmental science, computer science, engineering, medicine and AI. Members discuss important scientific ideas each week, then put them into practice through research groups, competitions, writing and video.
